# max_demand_monitoring

**Path**: `lib/web_app_template/max_demand_monitoring/`
**Category**: Energy

## Purpose
Monitors maximum demand (peak kW) over the billing cycle. Important for
TNB billing where exceeding contracted max demand incurs penalty charges.
Pairs with `tnb_e3_bill_simulator`.
