# oee_data

**Path**: `lib/web_app_template/oee_data/`
**Category**: Production

## Purpose
Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE) calculations and visualizations.
OEE = Availability × Performance × Quality. Aggregates data from
status, quantity, and quality logs to produce the standard OEE metric.
